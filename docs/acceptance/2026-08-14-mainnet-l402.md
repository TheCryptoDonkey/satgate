# Mainnet L402 acceptance — 14 August 2026

Status: **PASS**

This record covers two controlled, real-money Lightning payments against the
public Satgate service linked from the project README: one human Wallet of
Satoshi payment and one automated Rizful NWC payment. It separates field
evidence from unit, integration, release and deployment checks.

## Human-wallet safety envelope

- One 10-sat invoice was created for this acceptance run.
- The payer authorised at most 2 sats in routing fees.
- Only one payment attempt was made. There was no blind retry.
- The production applications held a limited Phoenixd credential and had no
  outgoing-payment permission.

## Automated NWC acceptance

A second run exercised the released automated client path:

`402-mcp → @forgesworn/nwc-kit → Rizful → Satgate → Phoenixd`

### Safety envelope

- A dedicated Rizful test vault held 20 sats before the run.
- `402-mcp` ran with `MAX_AUTO_PAY_SATS=10`,
  `MAX_SPEND_PER_MINUTE_SATS=10` and `FETCH_MAX_RETRIES=0`.
- The harness allowed one `autoPay` invocation and no blind payment retry.
- The NWC bearer URI was loaded through a temporary `0600` file. The temporary
  file and isolated encrypted L402 credential store were removed after use.

Satgate's 250-credit daily free allowance initially returned HTTP 200 without
payment. The client stopped before `autoPay`. After the remaining allowance for
the test IP was deliberately consumed with lightweight GET requests that did
not invoke the model, a dry run returned a fresh 10-sat HTTP 402 challenge.

preimage, L402 credential, Phoenixd credentials, wallet addresses and NWC

## What this proves

Together these runs prove two end-to-end mainnet paths. A human paid Satgate's
Lightning invoice from Wallet of Satoshi. Separately, the released `402-mcp`
client enforced its spend caps, paid one invoice automatically through Rizful
and `nwc-kit`, independently verified settlement, and used the resulting L402
credential. Satgate served paid inference in both cases, and the receiving node
accounted for each 10-sat payment.

It does not prove every wallet, route, model or payment rail; sustained
availability or throughput; or material-funds custody through a small test
vault.
